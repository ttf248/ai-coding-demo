Welcome to Pixel Flow

This interactive art experience transforms your images into a dynamic particle cloud. Use your hand as a 'magic brush' to paint the image back into existence.

How to Interact

1. Allow webcam access when prompted.

2. A small video of you will appear on the bottom-left.

3. Move your index finger in front of the camera to guide the particles back to their place.

Use your index finger to 'paint' the image.
